Norse Atlantic ASA has partnered with Pakistan International Airlines (PIA) for two Boeing 787-9 Dreamliners serving UK-Pakistan routes, according to the Norse release. The ACMI agreement is expected to start operations in November 2026. Under ACMI, Norse supplies the aircraft, crew, maintenance and insurance, and the planes fly for PIA.
