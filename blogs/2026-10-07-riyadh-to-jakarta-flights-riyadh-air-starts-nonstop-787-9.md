Riyadh Air is launching nonstop flights between King Khalid International Airport (RUH) and Jakarta’s Soekarno-Hatta International Airport (CGK), with ticket sales open for a 16 November 2026 start, Gulf Daily News reported on 5 October. The route becomes the airline’s 16th destination and is flown three times a week by Boeing 787-9 Dreamliners.
