Centrum Air will resume direct flights from Tashkent to Mattala Rajapaksa International Airport in Hambantota, Sri Lanka, on 28 October 2026. Centrum Air’s official news page confirms the route and start date. It reconnects Uzbekistan with southern Sri Lanka after a pause since January 2025, per AeroRoutes.
