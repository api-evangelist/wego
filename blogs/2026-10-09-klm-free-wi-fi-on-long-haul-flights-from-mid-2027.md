KLM will offer free high-speed Wi-Fi on intercontinental flights from mid-2027, announced on 8 October 2026. It is free for Flying Blue members and covers every cabin class on equipped aircraft. Passengers should confirm they have a Flying Blue account before flying intercontinental KLM routes from mid-2027.
