Each season that arrives in Azerbaijan asks you to look at the country differently. Autumn spreads gold and copper through the forests of the north, while the coast and capital stay mild enough for slow afternoons outdoors. Then winter settles over the mountains, turning those ranges into ski slopes and the villages into hushed retreats.
