President Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan has renamed Al Ain International Airport to Tahnoun International Airport, honoring the late Sheikh Tahnoun bin Mohammed Al Nahyan’s decades of service to the UAE. The new name took effect on 11 October 2026. It affects anyone flying to or from Al Ain.
