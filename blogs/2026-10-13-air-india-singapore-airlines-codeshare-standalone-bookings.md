Air India and Singapore Airlines (SIA) end bundled booking requirements starting 15 October 2026, the airlines said in a joint release. Travelers may book eligible India-Singapore flights separately on either carrier’s booking system. A codeshare flight is flown by one airline but also sold under its partner’s name.
